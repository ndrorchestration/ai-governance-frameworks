## Platinum Mean / Index-11 Research Anchor

For this project, the **Platinum Mean** notation is:

```text
pP = 1/(2 sin(π/11)) ≈ 1.774732842
```

This is the circumradius of a regular hendecagon with unit side length. The reciprocal geometric quantity

```text
2 sin(π/11) ≈ 0.563465...
```

is a different quantity and must not be labeled `pP`. The Platinum Mean `pP` must also not be conflated with the plastic constant `ρ ≈ 1.3247179572447454`.

The mathematical identity **does not establish** that `pP`, a hendecagonal geometry, an 11-node topology, or any derived schedule improves multi-agent stability, alignment, safety, hallucination rate, or governance quality. Those are empirical hypotheses requiring separate experiments.

## Evidence Boundary

The following are different evidence classes and must remain separate:

- **VERIFIABLE MATHEMATICS:** algebraic/geometric identities such as the definition/value of `pP`.
- **SUPPORTED ANALOGY:** a conceptual relationship useful for design or explanation but not proven equivalent.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** claims that a mathematical ratio, topology, threshold, or intervention affects an AI-system endpoint.
- **EMPIRICAL RESULT:** requires an authorized, executed, measured experiment with a defined analysis.

This document currently contains mathematics, analogies, and hypotheses. It does not contain an accepted empirical result establishing Index-11/Phi-based governance efficacy.
